# Liquid Glass Web

## Purpose

Use restrained glass surfaces to clarify floating or layered controls without weakening readability or turning ordinary content into decoration.

## Workflow

1. Confirm glass communicates layering or persistence better than a solid surface.
2. Read `design-system-tokens` for every glass, border, shadow, and layering value.
3. Prefer glass for navigation, floating search, dialogs, sheets, hero overlays, and floating toolbars.
4. Keep regular content cards solid unless the task provides a specific hierarchy reason.
5. Provide a readable solid fallback when backdrop filtering is unavailable or transparency would reduce clarity.
6. Review contrast across real backgrounds and all interactive states.

## Rules

- Do not define typography, branding, layout, responsive behavior, or exact token values.
- Do not nest multiple glass surfaces without a clear layer model.
- Do not place essential text over uncontrolled imagery without a readability layer.
- Do not add glass solely to make a surface look more futuristic.
- Prefer borders and restrained depth over excessive shadow.

## Outputs

Produce the justified glass surface hierarchy, token usage, fallback behavior, and readability evidence.
